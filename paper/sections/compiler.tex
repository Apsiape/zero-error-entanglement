\section{The compiler at a general modular ratio}\label{sec:compiler}
We use the finite projection and Boolean-row compiler of \cite[Section~4]{Sigma2}, itself based on \cite{Entropy}. Its graph identities, rational-row tests, and normal support proofs remain unchanged. We specify the parameter change and prove its consequences. Each flagged-magic-square (FMS) bridge forces a density ratio of two between consecutive selected corners (Figure~\ref{fig:chain}).
A chain of $n$ bridges therefore gives the ratio $2^n$ from source to far corner.
Transporting a source-to-far creation operator back to the source gives $\lambda=2^{-n}$.
Four orthogonal creations then generate a rooted tree with four branches per vertex.
The density of each word sector is $\lambda^{|w|}$ times the root density.
Summing over the $4^\ell$ words at depth $\ell$ gives a geometric depth distribution.
The exact spectrum identity below accounts for the finite scaffold and auxiliary factors.
Its square-root trace determines whether perfect play gives a normal channel code.

\subsection{Admissible input and the \texorpdfstring{$n$}{n}-bridge assembly}
\begin{definition}\label{def:admissible}
Fix an integer $n\geq3$ and put $\lambda=2^{-n}$. An admissible presentation consists of four contraction symbols $A_\alpha$, $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^2$, a centralizer root projection $P_\circ$, and finitely many centralizer labels typed as unitaries, projections, positive contractions, or general contractions. Besides rational $*$-polynomials in the centralizer labels, its equations are
\[
 A_\alpha^*A_\beta=\delta_{\alpha\beta}I,\qquad
 \sum_\alpha A_\alpha A_\alpha^*=I-P_\circ,
\]
endpoint equations $LA_\alpha=A_\beta B$ or $LA_\alpha=0$, and finite sums $\sum_\nu B_\nu A_{\alpha_\nu}C_\nu=0$. The endpoint coefficients are centralizer polynomials; every label appearing at a left endpoint is transported. No other monomial contains more than one creation symbol.

A normal realization is a nonzero Hilbert space with a faithful density $h_0$, bounded operators of the declared types, all the application identities, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:covariance}
 [h_0,L]=0\quad\text{for centralizer labels},\qquad
 h_0A_\alpha=\lambda A_\alpha h_0.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
The change from \cite[Definition in Section~4.4]{Sigma2} is the number of bridges and the covariance ratio. In particular the ratio is imposed by the assembly, rather than by a real coefficient in the Boolean game.

Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weights}
 \Omega_n=1+4\sum_{g=1}^n2^g=2^{n+3}-7,\qquad
 \mu_n=\Omega_n^{-1}(1;2^{\oplus4};4^{\oplus4};\ldots;(2^n)^{\oplus4}).
\end{equation}
The notation $t^{\oplus4}$ denotes four entries equal to $t$.

\begin{lemma}[$n$-bridge compiler]\label{lem:compiler}
Every admissible presentation effectively gives a finite rational Boolean game $G^{(n)}$. Perfect normal spatial strategies exist exactly when normal realizations exist. The completeness map appends finite scaffold, qutrit, and row factors. The assembly parameters are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}\toprule
quantity&value\\\midrule
orthogonal scaffold corners&$4n+1$\\
frame-bound parameters&$6n+1$\\
cover constant&$\Omega_n=2^{n+3}-7$\\
chain covariance&$hv=2^n vh$\\
normalized source density&$\Omega_n e_0he_0$\\
far-corner weight relative to source&$2^n$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here $h$ is the normalized scaffold density after removing its finite scalar factors. The completeness construction preserves finite marginal entropy.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use mutually orthogonal synchronized corners
\[
 e_0;\quad m_{g,00},m_{g,01},m_{g,10},m_{g,11}\quad(1\leq g\leq n),
\]
put $e_g=m_{g,00}$ and $M_g=\sum_{a,b}m_{g,ab}$, and impose $e_0+\sum_gM_g=I$. Conditional flagged-magic-square bridge $g$ has flag $e_{g-1}$, magic support $M_g$, and activity $e_{g-1}+M_g$. Retain the original questions, all activity/event bindings in both orientations, and the label refinements of \cite[Lemmas~4.1--4.2]{Sigma2}. The normal bridge gives partial isometries
\[
 w_g^*w_g=e_{g-1},\qquad w_gw_g^*=e_g,\qquad hw_g=2w_gh.
\]
The other three magic corners are identified by synchronized Pauli matrix units and have the same density block as $e_g$. Thus $v=w_n\cdots w_1$ satisfies
\[
 v^*v=e_0,\qquad vv^*=e_n,\qquad hv=2^n vh.
\]
Cyclicity of trace makes each selected corner have mass $2^g$ times the source mass. The cover gives source mass $1/\Omega_n$, hence a nonzero source. There are $4n+1$ corners; adding the two positive Pauli-axis events for each bridge gives $6n+1$ parameters bound to the scalar qutrit frame.

Here are the remaining equations, to fix the assembly unambiguously. A unitary label uses itself, a projection uses $2P-I$, a positive contraction $T$ uses $(2I+u+u^*)/4$, and a general contraction uses $(u_++u_-)/2$. Let $\Lambda$ contain the encoding of $P_\circ$ and all unitary encodings required at left endpoints. The root projection is transported even when it occurs in no endpoint equation. For $0\leq r\leq n$ impose
\[
 (u_{r,t}-I)(I-e_r)=0\quad(t\in\Lambda).
\]
Decode the corner copies $L_r$ using the corner identity $e_r$. For each bridge impose the block-unitary equation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blockunitary}
 v_{g,t}=I-e_{g-1}-M_g+e_{g-1}u_{g-1,t}
 +\sum_{a,b}\mathfrak x_g^a\mathfrak z_g^be_gu_{g,t}\mathfrak z_g^{-b}\mathfrak x_g^{-a}.
\end{equation}
Here $\mathfrak x_g,\mathfrak z_g$ are the bound Pauli reflections extended by identity outside $M_g$. Refine each retained sign question, on each party, for the six frame projections
\[
 F_1,F_2,F_3,G_{12}(1),G_{13}(1),G_{23}(1)
\]
and for $G_{12}(v_{g,t})$, $t\in\Lambda$. The shared opposite questions force every refined coarse sign to equal the original one. The six frame commutators give $w_g=I_3\otimes\widetilde w_g$. The graph-label commutator then gives the actual transport equation
\[
 w_gu_{g-1,t}=u_{g,t}w_g,\qquad L_nv=vL_0.
\]
Distinct transported labels need not commute with each other.

Introduce centralizer contractions $a_\alpha=e_na_\alpha e_0$, encoded by two unitaries. Translate the application equations as follows:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}\toprule
root equation&centralizer equation\\\midrule
$A_\alpha^*A_\beta=\delta_{\alpha\beta}I$&$a_\alpha^*a_\beta=\delta_{\alpha\beta}e_0$\\
$\sum_\alpha A_\alpha A_\alpha^*=I-P_\circ$&$\sum_\alpha a_\alpha a_\alpha^*=e_n-(P_\circ)_n$\\
$LA_\alpha=A_\beta B$&$L_na_\alpha=a_\beta B_0$\\
$LA_\alpha=0$&$L_na_\alpha=0$\\
$\sum_\nu B_\nu A_{\alpha_\nu}C_\nu=0$&$\sum_\nu (B_\nu)_na_{\alpha_\nu}(C_\nu)_0=0$\\
centralizer polynomial&same polynomial in source copies\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Extraction $A_\alpha=v^*a_\alpha$ reverses every row, since $v$ is unitary between the two corners and intertwines all left endpoint labels. It gives \eqref{eq:covariance}, because $hv^*=2^{-n}v^*h$. Only the displayed centralizer polynomials are submitted to the graph compiler; products of modular transports are never treated as centralizer words.

For completeness, from a realization take the scaffold density
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scaffolddensity}
 h_{\rm sc}=\operatorname{diag}(\mu_n)\otimes h_0.
\end{equation}
Use identity corner identifications and put $a_\alpha=|e_n\rangle\langle e_0|\otimes A_\alpha$. The covariance makes $a_\alpha$ commute with $h_{\rm sc}$. Its positive eigenspaces are finite dimensional. Within each such block the polar partial isometry of any contraction $T$ extends to a unitary $V$, and
\[
 U_\pm=V\bigl(|T|\pm i\sqrt{I-|T|^2}\bigr),\qquad T=(U_++U_-)/2.
\]
Their direct sums are bounded centralizer unitaries. Positive contractions use $u=2T-I+2i\sqrt{T(I-T)}$; projections use their reflections. Copy the same source encodings through the bridge identifications. This realizes all block and support equations simultaneously.

Append a maximally entangled qutrit and apply the actual graph and triangle formulas of \cite[Section~4.2]{Sigma2}. They certify products of the common labels. Finally apply the Boolean rational-row construction \cite[Lemma~4.3]{Sigma2}, adjoining a finite maximally entangled row ancilla of dimension $\drow=d_{\max}$. Its old-projection/scalar-axis joint questions put every named synchronized old projection in the commutant of $\mathrm{Mat}_{\drow}$. The FMS and refinement tests remain in the game. These unchanged normal support arguments prove both implications. All new indices are finite; the coefficients and sampling probabilities are rational. Padding questions and accepting unspecified pairs gives a finite game effectively. The appended factors have finite entropy.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An exact spectrum identity}
One must be careful when removing the row factor in soundness: the FMS signs were not synchronized, so their transports need not commute with $\mathrm{Mat}_{\drow}$. The following elementary cancellation supplies the needed reduction without a partial trace over an infinite space.

\paragraph{Finite-amplification cancellation.}
If two finite-dimensional tuples of unitaries become unitarily equivalent after $\drow$ identical copies, the original tuples are unitarily equivalent. Indeed they are finite-dimensional representations of the universal $*$-algebra on the tuple. The image in their direct sum is a finite-dimensional $C^*$-algebra. Decompose its two representations into irreducibles; amplification multiplies every multiplicity by $\drow$, so equality of amplified multiplicities cancels $\drow$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact spectrum identity]\label{lem:spectrum}
For every pure perfect strategy of $G^{(n)}$, with marginal $h$ and Schmidt operator $D=\sqrt h$, there is a normal realization with density $h_0$ such that, including multiplicities,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:specidentity}
 \spec(h)=\spec\left(\operatorname{diag}(\mu_n)\otimes h_0
                    \otimes I_3/3\otimes I_{\drow}/\drow\right).
\end{equation}
Conversely every realization has a perfect strategy with this spectrum. In particular, in the extended nonnegative reals,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:traceidentity}
 \Tr D=c_n\Tr\sqrt{h_0},\qquad
 c_n=\sqrt{3\drow}\,\frac{1+4\sum_{g=1}^n2^{g/2}}{\sqrt{\Omega_n}}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Restrict to the original marginal support. Synchronization makes the certified scalar matrix algebras commute with the density. The named old projections commute with $\mathrm{Mat}_{\drow}$; the bound parameters and graph units also provide the scalar $\mathrm{Mat}_3$ frame. These two scalar algebras commute, so their exact matrix-unit decompositions give a density $I_3/3\otimes I_{\drow}/\drow\otimes h_{\rm base}$ and factorizations of every named old synchronized projection. This is a finite matrix-unit decomposition, not an uncontrolled partial trace.

The magic matrix units identify all four density blocks at each bridge. The $w_g$ identify consecutive normalized selected density blocks, with ratio two. The cover therefore implies that the full density has the spectrum in \eqref{eq:specidentity}, where $h_0$ is its normalized source block after removal of both scalar factors. It remains to verify that this $h_0$ is the density of a realization; simply compressing $v^*a_\alpha$ across $\mathrm{Mat}_{\drow}$ would not prove that assertion.

The frame refinements make $v$ act trivially on $\mathrm{Mat}_3$. After removing that frame it is a unitary between the $\drow$-amplifications of the source and far spaces, intertwining their normalized densities and every transported unitary label. In each positive density eigenspace these spaces are finite dimensional. Apply finite-amplification cancellation to their transported unitary tuples. This gives an unamplified unitary for each positive eigenvalue. Their Hilbert direct sum $\widetilde v$ intertwines $h_0$ with the normalized far density, and intertwines all transported labels simultaneously. The densities are faithful, so these eigenspaces exhaust both spaces.

All centralizer contractions $a_\alpha$ are polynomials in old labels and factor across both scalar algebras; write their reduced forms $\widetilde a_\alpha$. Define
$\widetilde A_\alpha=\widetilde v^*\widetilde a_\alpha$.
The reduced translated equations in Lemma~\ref{lem:compiler} give every application identity. Intertwining of the normalized densities, together with the source/far weight $2^n$, gives $h_0\widetilde A_\alpha=2^{-n}\widetilde A_\alpha h_0$. This proves the realization assertion. The new creations need not be restrictions of the original creations.

Completeness uses \eqref{eq:scaffolddensity}, followed by the two normalized maximally mixed factors, so has exactly the same spectrum. Taking the square root and the trace of this finite tensor product proves \eqref{eq:traceidentity}; nonnegative spectral sums justify it even when the answer is infinity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The tree at general \texorpdfstring{$\lambda$}{lambda}}\label{sec:tree}
The tree part of \cite[Section~5]{Sigma2} works for every $0<\lambda<1/4$. For clarity we give the density calculation. For a word $w$ in four letters, write $A_w$ for the product of its creations and
\[
 Q_\ell=\sum_{|w|=\ell}A_wA_w^*.
\]
The isometry relations and covariance imply
\[
 \Tr(h_0Q_\ell)=(4\lambda)^\ell,\qquad
 \Tr(h_0P_\circ)=1-4\lambda.
\]
The decreasing $Q_\ell$ have a strong limit of zero $h_0$-weight, hence zero by faithfulness. Their successive differences are $\sum_{|w|=\ell}A_wP_\circ A_w^*$. Thus, with $\mathcal K=P_\circ\HH\ne0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:treefactor}
 \HH\simeq\ell^2(\WW)\otimes\mathcal K,\quad
 h_0=h_{F,\lambda}\otimes\sigma,\quad
 h_{F,\lambda}=(1-4\lambda)\sum_w\lambda^{|w|}|w\rangle\langle w|,
\end{equation}
where $\sigma$ is a faithful density. The covariance makes every word-sector projection commute with $h_0$, so transporting the root block proves the equality, not only its diagonal entries. Depth $\ell$ has probability $(1-4\lambda)(4\lambda)^\ell$; $(4\lambda)^\ell$ is the mass at depth at least $\ell$.

The coordinate, Weyl-clock, bounded period-reader and deterministic tableau equations of \cite[Sections~5--7]{Sigma2} contain no Fock density ratio. Their proofs use only faithful normality, positive finite-dimensional density eigenspaces, the exact tree decomposition, and a positive weight at every finite depth. All hold in \eqref{eq:treefactor}. Consequently their soundness and canonical completeness hold unchanged for every $\lambda=2^{-n}<1/4$. In particular the Weyl determinant still forces a root phase of finite order: on a positive density eigenspace of dimension $\qclk$, $J_{\rm clk}K_{\rm clk}=\zeta K_{\rm clk}J_{\rm clk}$ gives $\zeta^{\qclk}=1$. No clock relation is squared or otherwise changed.

\subsection{Summability and its critical point}
Equation~\eqref{eq:treefactor} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trsqrtF}
 \Tr\sqrt{h_{F,\lambda}}
 =\sqrt{1-4\lambda}\sum_{\ell=0}^\infty(4\sqrt\lambda)^\ell
 =\frac{\sqrt{1-4\lambda}}{1-4\sqrt\lambda}
\end{equation}
when $\lambda<1/16$, and infinity when $1/16\leq\lambda<1/4$.
For the canonical YES realization $\sigma=I_{\qclk}/\qclk$, Lemma~\ref{lem:spectrum} therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:YESsum}
 \Tr D=\sqrt{3\qclk\drow}\,
 \frac{1+4\sum_{g=1}^n2^{g/2}}{\sqrt{\Omega_n}}
 \frac{\sqrt{1-2^{2-n}}}{1-2^{2-n/2}}\quad(n>4).
\end{equation}
For five bridges and the optional eight-bridge construction, the exact coefficients are
\begin{align}
 n=5:&\quad \Tr D=\frac{(81+53\sqrt2)\sqrt7}{2\sqrt{83}}\sqrt{\qclk\drow},\label{eq:fiveconstant}\\
 n=8:&\quad \Tr D=\frac{(121+60\sqrt2)\sqrt{21}}{2\sqrt{2041}}\sqrt{\qclk\drow}.\label{eq:eightconstant}
\end{align}
They are approximately $22.65\sqrt{\qclk\drow}$ and $10.44\sqrt{\qclk\drow}$.

\begin{remark}[The original four bridges are critical]\label{rem:critical}
At $n=4$, every realization has $\Tr\sqrt{h_0}=\infty$, regardless of $\sigma$, because the per-depth amplitude sum in \eqref{eq:trsqrtF} is constant and nonzero. Lemma~\ref{lem:spectrum} then makes every perfect pure game strategy have infinite amplitude sum. Theorem~\ref{thm:correspondence} implies that the channel of every game $G_e$ of \cite[Theorem~7.1]{Sigma2} has no $k$-message normal code, for every input index. Applying its original games directly would therefore give an empty YES set, rather than the desired hardness reduction.
\end{remark}
