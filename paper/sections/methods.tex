\section*{How this work was done}\label{sec:methods}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How this work was done}

The authors selected the questions and proof standards, directed the research, and take responsibility for every claim. AI systems from more than one developer assisted with exploration, derivation, programming and drafting.

The mathematical evidence consists of the proofs in this paper and the explicitly cited external theorems. The ancillary programs provide reproducible finite checks. Independent reviews examined the operator arguments, attribution, complexity reductions and entropy calculations. The finite programs test strategy and code matrices, exact bridge constants, spectrum identities and Catalan generating-function identities. Their arithmetic and scope are stated in Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}; numerical checks are distinguished from exact checks.

These finite checks do not prove infinite-dimensional existence, compactness or arithmetical hardness by sampling. Those conclusions rest on the written proofs and the cited source reductions. This preprint has not been peer reviewed.
